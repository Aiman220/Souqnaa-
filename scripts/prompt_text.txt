3. Universal Other / أخرى implementation

Every one of the 20 main categories must have exactly one active Other subcategory.

When a seller selects Other:

- Display a required field:
  - English: Specify product type
  - Arabic: حدد نوع المنتج
- Accept Arabic and English text.
- Trim whitespace and reject empty or whitespace-only values.
- Enforce reasonable minimum and maximum length limits.
- Store the submitted value separately from the official subcategory ID.
- Preserve the selected main category and Other subcategory relationship.
- Display the entered product type in seller product management and customer product details where appropriate.
- Include the value in product search and filtering.
- Make the entry available for admin review.
- Allow authorized admins to approve, reject, correct, or promote recurring product types into official subcategories through the existing category-management workflow.
- Preserve the original submitted value and moderation history in the audit trail.
- Do not automatically create global subcategories from seller input.
- Prevent duplicate Other subcategories under the same parent.
- Do not allow sellers to approve their own submissions or change official category definitions.

Selecting Other under Digital Products must not change digital fulfillment or create a physical delivery charge.
